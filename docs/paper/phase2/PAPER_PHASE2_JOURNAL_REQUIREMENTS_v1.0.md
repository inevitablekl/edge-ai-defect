## 3. Verified Requirements

| Topic | Verified official requirement | Phase 2 handling |
|---|---|---|
| Chinese title | Generally no more than 20 Chinese characters. | Keep title compact and perform a final Word-layout check. |
| Subtitle | Generally not used. | No subtitle is planned. |
| Bilingual front matter | Chinese and English titles, author names, affiliations, locations/postcodes, abstracts, and keywords are required. | Create paired Chinese/English fields during drafting; author fields remain outside the anonymous copy. |
| Chinese abstract | A report-style abstract is preferred, normally at least 150 Chinese characters; it should cover purpose, method, result, and conclusion, be self-contained, use third person, and avoid figure/table/formula/reference numbers. | Phase 2 records an abstract packet only; no abstract prose is drafted here. |
| Keywords | At least 4 Chinese keywords; English keywords correspond one-to-one, and an English abbreviation should first give its full form. | Plan 4-6 controlled terms and verify one-to-one translation. |
| Article length | Generally within 10,000 Chinese characters, covering introduction, analysis, results, and conclusion. | Internal target is below the ceiling with a margin for layout revision. |
| Recent work | The introduction should especially reflect research from the latest 2 years. | Literature requirements include a 2024-2026 recent-work lane, based on the current verification date. |
| Abbreviations | Non-common foreign abbreviations should give a Chinese translation or foreign-language full form at first occurrence. | Maintain a terminology/first-use checklist. |
| Quantities and units | Use current national legal measurement units; distinguish case, upright/italic, weight, superscripts, and subscripts. | FPS, ms, image counts, and ratios remain distinct; final formula typography awaits the attachment. |
| Figures and tables | Must be self-explanatory and clear; text, symbols, and axis values must be explicit and consistent with the article. | Every candidate records axes, quantities, units, source metrics, and limitations. |
| Numeric labels in figures/tables | Values must have explicit quantities and units. | Use `FPS` for frame rate and `ms` for latency; ratios and percentages are labeled separately. |
| Tables | Use three-line tables. | All article tables are planned as three-line tables. |
| References | Use sequential numeric citation order; cite recent domestic, international, and journal work; generally at least 8 references. | Build a requirements list first; do not fabricate entries. |
| Original and review files | Original and review versions are Word documents. | Markdown assets are planning inputs only; final submission conversion is a later manual/drafting task. |
| Anonymous review | The review version must remove author-related information. | Maintain separate full and anonymous Word copies; remove author names, affiliations, funding, biography, and contact information from the anonymous copy as applicable. |

## 7. Submission Checklist for the Later Word Stage

- Confirm the four pending attachments and record actual filenames, types,
  exact bytes, and SHA256.
- Apply verified typography and figure/table settings in Word.
- Prepare Chinese/English title, abstract, and keyword pairs.
- Keep the Chinese abstract at or above 150 characters without drafting claims
  beyond the Phase 1 freeze.
- Keep the article within 10,000 Chinese characters and preserve revision
  margin.
- Use at least 4 Chinese keywords and generally at least 8 real, read sources.
- Use sequential reference numbering and three-line tables.
- Produce both original and anonymous-review Word files; inspect the anonymous
  file and document properties for author-identifying information.
