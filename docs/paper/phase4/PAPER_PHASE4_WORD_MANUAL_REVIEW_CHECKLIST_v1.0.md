# Paper Phase 4.9 Windows Word Manual Review Checklist

Status: `USER_MANUAL_EXECUTION_REQUIRED`

Use untouched copies of `draft_full.docx` and `draft_anonymous.docx`. Record every
failure before making any Word-side correction.

## A. Full DOCX

- [ ] Open without a repair or unreadable-content warning.
- [ ] Save as a temporary review copy, close it, and reopen it normally.
- [ ] Check Chinese and English title wrapping and the complete bilingual front matter.
- [ ] Check authors, affiliations, and both corresponding-author lines; confirm no email was invented.
- [ ] Confirm the accepted biography appears once in the first-page footer and nowhere in the body.
- [ ] Confirm page 1 and later pages display stable continuous page numbers.
- [ ] Confirm the body begins correctly and uses two columns outside the F1 span.
- [ ] Check heading hierarchy, numbering, and no-wrap behavior.
- [ ] Check F1 at its full-width placement for actual text and line readability.
- [ ] Check F2/F3 fonts, labels, error bars, cropping, aspect ratio, and readability.
- [ ] Check all figure captions, including the F2 sample-SD and F3 pooled-sample explanations in the surrounding accepted text.
- [ ] Check T1/T2 three-line borders, wrapping, row integrity, and page-break behavior.
- [ ] Check reference typography, hanging indents, URLs, and the last page.
- [ ] Confirm there is no blank page or visibly broken section transition.

## B. Anonymous DOCX

- [ ] Open without repair warning; save a temporary copy, close, and reopen it.
- [ ] Confirm no visible author, affiliation, corresponding-author, biography, email, or identity-bearing footer remains.
- [ ] Confirm scientific formatting matches Full apart from identity-only natural reflow.
- [ ] Confirm identity removal leaves no conspicuously broken blank gaps.
- [ ] Confirm there is no blank page.

## C. Word Document Inspector

- [ ] Inspect comments and tracked revisions.
- [ ] Inspect document properties and personal information.
- [ ] Inspect headers, footers, and hidden text.
- [ ] Inspect custom XML and custom properties where Word surfaces them.
- [ ] Record the Inspector result separately for Full and Anonymous.

## D. Field Refresh

- [ ] In a temporary copy, use `Ctrl+A`, then `F9`.
- [ ] Confirm PAGE fields update safely and numbering remains continuous.
- [ ] Confirm `参考文献` remains unnumbered after refresh.
- [ ] Save, close, and reopen once more; confirm the result remains stable.

## E. Failure Record

For each failure record:

```text
document:
page:
object:
symptom:
screenshot/manual note:
```

Do not apply an unrecorded ad hoc Word fix to the review candidates.
