## 3. Phase 5.4B controls

Phase 5.4B must verify bibliographic identity and claim-level support before admission, prefer primary sources, retain conservative scope, and avoid using external literature to retrofit causal explanations for unmeasured project behavior. A candidate may be rejected or replaced if its evidence does not support the exact claim.
